Model & prototype details

LP-Distilled 0.1 renders 256 × 256 frames from your cropped image, pose, and expression controls. The first load downloads the model and ONNX Runtime from this site. WebGPU is used when supported; the CPU preview can take several seconds per frame. This prototype tests facial motion; it doesn’t yet perform speech-synchronized lip animation or full 3D head reconstruction.
