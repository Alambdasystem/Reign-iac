02 / Born from passion

Passion is
our starting
point.

We believe driving should be more than transportation. It should be an expression of individuality, freedom, innovation, and ambition.

Christian Motors Group is an emerging American automotive venture focused on distinctive, limited-production luxury performance vehicles. We are starting deliberately—with a vision, a disciplined development plan, and the intention to build something enduring.

I

Purpose over volume

We'd rather build a small number of meaningful machines than chase mass-market scale at launch.

II

Innovation without compromise

Technology must serve the driver, the experience, and the long-term product vision.

III

Legacy through craftsmanship

Thoughtful design, committed partners, and measured execution are the foundation for what comes next.

03 / The road ahead

Built with patience.
Driven by ambition.

Our ambition is an initial production run of approximately ten roadsters during our first two production years. We know the difference between a compelling concept and a road-ready product. The work ahead is engineering, validation, financing, and execution.

STAGE 01

Supply chain & costs

Identify qualified engineering and manufacturing partners. Establish scope, vendor pricing, and a credible prototype budget.

STAGE 02

Prototype engineering

Resolve hybrid architecture, powertrain, battery capacity, safety requirements, and build a demonstrator.

STAGE 03

Test & validate

Evaluate performance, durability, range, emissions, roadworthiness, and regulatory compliance.

STAGE 04

Limited production

Explore a tightly controlled first series after securing funding, technical validation, and manufacturing readiness.
